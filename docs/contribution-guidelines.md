# Contribution Guidelines

## Purpose

The IPI Research Platform is intended to support serious, structured, and collaborative research development across disciplines.

The objective is not simply to publish ideas, but to make them understandable, navigable, and capable of meaningful interaction with other work.

Contributors are encouraged to prioritize:

- clarity,
- intellectual honesty,
- structural consistency,
- openness to revision,
- and collaborative integration.

---

# Core Principles

## Clarity Over Performance

The goal is understanding, not intellectual theater.

Complex ideas are welcome.
Unnecessary obscurity is not.

Contributors are encouraged to:

- define terminology,
- separate intuition from formalism,
- explain assumptions,
- identify uncertainty clearly,
- and communicate as accessibly as possible without sacrificing rigor.

---

## Integration Over Duplication

Whenever possible:

> integrate before restarting.

Before creating entirely new structures, contributors are encouraged to:

- examine related projects,
- identify conceptual overlap,
- reference existing work,
- and build bridges where appropriate.

The platform is designed to reduce unnecessary fragmentation.

---

## Explicit Uncertainty

Open questions are not considered weaknesses.

Research is often exploratory, incomplete, and evolving.

Contributors are encouraged to identify:

- limitations,
- unresolved problems,
- ambiguities,
- contradictory implications,
- and speculative areas explicitly.

Artificial certainty is discouraged.

---

## Respect for Formalism

The platform welcomes:

- conceptual work,
- mathematical work,
- computational work,
- philosophical work,
- and interdisciplinary exploration.

However:

claims presented as formal structures should strive for actual structural rigor.

Contributors should distinguish clearly between:

- intuition,
- interpretation,
- speculation,
- and formal derivation.

Saying “this is incomplete” is completely acceptable.

Pretending incompleteness is rigor is not.

---

# Project Contributions

## Local Project Structures

Each project may define:

- its own terminology,
- notation,
- assumptions,
- conceptual framework,
- and organizational structure.

The platform does not impose a universal ontology across all projects.

However, contributors should strive for:

- internal consistency,
- explicit definitions,
- and conceptual clarity.

---

## Suggested Contribution Areas

Contributions may include:

- conceptual clarification,
- mathematical derivations,
- computational implementations,
- simulations,
- references,
- literature connections,
- explanatory notes,
- validation attempts,
- or structural criticism.

---

## Criticism

Critical engagement is encouraged.

However:

- criticism should target structures and arguments,
- not individuals,
- status,
- or disciplines.

The objective is collaborative refinement rather than adversarial performance.

---

# Research Notes

The Notes section exists for:

- exploratory writing,
- essays,
- conceptual bridges,
- explanatory articles,
- and developing intuitions.

Notes do not require the same level of formal maturity as project pages.

However, contributors should still strive for:

- coherence,
- clarity,
- and meaningful structure.

---

# Attribution

Contributors should:

- reference related work where appropriate,
- avoid presenting existing ideas as original,
- and distinguish clearly between personal contributions and inherited structures.

The platform encourages integration, not ownership performance.

---

# Final Principle

The platform exists to support meaningful inquiry.

The objective is not to win arguments, construct intellectual identities, or simulate profundity.

The objective is to help ideas become clear enough that they can be explored, challenged, tested, improved, and connected with the work of others.

Which, unfortunately, requires humans communicating with each other in good faith. A historically unstable system, but we persist.
